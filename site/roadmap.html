Next, in this order

  1. Password reset and email delivery; a session management screen.
  2. Continuous integration and a confirmed working Docker build.
  3. Fee schedules, invoices and manual payment recording with reconciliation.
  4. Guardian notifications (email first) and announcements.
  5. Timetable, holidays and academic calendar; reports and exports.
  6. Native wrappers built and tested.
  7. A specialist security review and accessibility audit before any claim of production readiness.

Not planned yet

Grading, learning content and QR check-in wait until real schools confirm what they need. Dates are not promised: this is a small open-source project.
