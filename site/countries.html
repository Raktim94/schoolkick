Countries and compatibility

SchoolKick is designed for institutions in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, India, Japan and Spain. Design is not the same as support. This page says what actually works today.

We use four words. Available: a country pack passed its acceptance checks. Pilot: running with a real institution under supervision. In development: some pieces work and the rest is being built. Not yet available: nothing country-specific exists. Today no market is Available or Pilot. We do not claim compliance, official integrations or data residency for any of them, and nothing here is legal advice.

What already works in any country

Learners, guardians and classes, attendance with a kept correction history, fees with cash receipts and PDF receipts that can be verified by QR, refunds with approval, and exam registration with fees. Any ISO currency can be set per institution. UPI payment QR codes work for Indian rupee invoices only. The interface is English only for now; Spanish, Hindi, Japanese and French are planned, and regional translations will need qualified review.

No national identifier such as SSN, Aadhaar or APAAR is required to use SchoolKick.
