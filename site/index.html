The work it does today

A register that keeps the whole story.

SchoolKick is an early, self-hosted attendance release. It keeps the day’s essential work together and makes the record easier to trust.

  1. 01

    Take attendance quickly

    Mark a whole class present, then change the exceptions. A draft survives a dropped connection.

    Attendance ↗
  2. 02

    Correct without erasing

    A saved mark can be changed with a reason. The earlier entry stays in the history with who changed it and when.

    Correction history ↗
  3. 03

    Let families check in

    Guardians see today’s status and the last two weeks in a browser. There is no app to install.

    Family view ↗
  4. 04

    Keep classes and records together

    Manage classes, learner profiles and guardian contacts. Check every CSV row before importing it.

Clear scope

Useful now. Honest about what comes later.

The current release focuses on attendance, learner and class records, a guardian view, CSV import, and an owner overview.

Fees and payments, messaging, timetables, QR check-in, learning materials and native mobile apps are planned; they are not part of this release.
